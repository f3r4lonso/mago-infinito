# Invocadas, hoja 2: criaturas y objetos desplegables

**Adjunta como referencia de estilo:** `mago_hoja1.png`, `torre_hoja1.png` y la hoja 1 de invocadas
**Formato:** 3:2 horizontal, cuadrícula de 6 columnas × 4 filas (24 celdas; se usan 16 y las 8 últimas quedan vacías)

```
Same art style as the attached reference sheets (original cartoon fantasy game art, bold
thick dark-purple outline, vivid saturated colors, glossy painted shading, light from
top-left). Plain solid magenta #FF00FF background.

UNIT SPRITE SHEET: 6 columns x 4 rows of equal cells separated by thin dark-purple grid
lines. Each UNIT uses TWO ADJACENT CELLS (left cell = frame A, right cell = frame B), so each
row holds 3 units. Every unit is an ALLIED, friendly-looking summoned creature or object,
chibi-proportioned, facing RIGHT, centered and filling about 80% of its cell, nothing
touching the cell edges. Frame A and B are the same unit in two poses (other leg forward,
wings up vs down) or, for objects, two states (idle vs active). NO letters, NO numbers, NO
labels, NO text, NO ground shadows.

Row 1: [1] SPIRIT BEAR: big translucent blue-white bear spirit, glowing eyes and paws, running |
       [2] SHADOW CLONE: dark-grey hooded ninja silhouette with a purple glow and two small daggers, like a smoky copy |
       [3] HAWK: a small sharp hawk with spread wings flying to the right (A = wings up, B = wings down)
Row 2: [4] DWARF TURRET: small brass-and-iron auto-turret on a tripod with a short barrel (A = idle, B = firing with a muzzle flash) |
       [5] LAND MINE: round flat metal mine with spikes and a small LED light (A = idle with a green light, B = armed with a red light) |
       [6] TROLL TOTEM: tall wooden tribal totem pole with a carved face and feathers (A = idle, B = glowing green)
Row 3: [7] THUNDER TOTEM: stone-and-wood totem with a blue crystal on top (A = idle, B = crackling with lightning) |
       [8] TIDE TOTEM: carved wooden totem with a blue water droplet crest (A = idle, B = glowing blue with water drops) |
       [empty] [empty] [empty] [empty]
Row 4: all 6 cells empty
```
