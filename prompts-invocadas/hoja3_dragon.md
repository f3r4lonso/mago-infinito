# Invocadas, hoja 3: el dragón (5 niveles de color), el huevo y el dragón esqueleto

**Adjunta como referencia de estilo:** `mago_hoja1.png`, `torre_hoja1.png` y la hoja 1 de invocadas
**Formato:** 3:2 horizontal, cuadrícula de 6 columnas × 4 filas (24 celdas; se usan 17)

El dragón sube de nivel y cambia de color en 5 etapas. Se pide el **mismo dragón** repetido con los 5 colores, para que el juego los cambie al subir.

```
Same art style as the attached reference sheets (original cartoon fantasy game art, bold
thick dark-purple outline, vivid saturated colors, glossy painted shading, light from
top-left). Plain solid magenta #FF00FF background.

DRAGON SPRITE SHEET: 6 columns x 4 rows of equal cells separated by thin dark-purple grid
lines. Allied pet dragon, chibi-proportioned (big head, small body), facing RIGHT, centered
and filling about 85% of its cell, nothing touching the cell edges. NO letters, NO numbers,
NO labels, NO text, NO ground shadows. The dragon is always the SAME design in every cell, only
the color changes.

CELLS in reading order:
1 dragon egg: cream egg with colored spots, intact
2 dragon egg: the same egg, cracked, with a bright glow inside
3 GREEN dragon, wings UP | 4 the same green dragon, wings DOWN
5 BLUE dragon, wings UP | 6 the same blue dragon, wings DOWN
7 PURPLE dragon, wings UP | 8 the same purple dragon, wings DOWN
9 RED dragon, wings UP | 10 the same red dragon, wings DOWN
11 GOLDEN dragon, wings UP (the strongest, with a crest and golden glow) | 12 the same golden dragon, wings DOWN
13 SKELETON dragon, wings UP: the same dragon design as bones with glowing teal eyes and ragged bone wings | 14 SKELETON dragon, wings DOWN
15 dragon fire breath: a cone of flames pointing right, narrow at the left tip and wide at the right
16 dragon bite: a sharp white-and-red double slash with fang marks
17 dragon tail swipe: a wide curved arc slash to the right
18-24: empty
```

**Recorte de archivos:** `dr_egg_1`, `dr_egg_2`, `dr_t1_1`, `dr_t1_2` (verde), `dr_t2_*` (azul), `dr_t3_*` (morado), `dr_t4_*` (rojo), `dr_t5_*` (dorado), `dr_skel_1`, `dr_skel_2`, `dr_fx_breath`, `dr_fx_bite`, `dr_fx_tail`.

**Consejos:** el dragón del juego aletea, vuela sobre el suelo y se voltea por código, así que importan sobre todo las **dos poses de alas**. Pídele a Gemini que la silueta sea idéntica en los 5 colores; si cambia, repite solo esa pareja de celdas.
