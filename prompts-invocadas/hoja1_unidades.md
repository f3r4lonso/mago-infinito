# Invocadas, hoja 1: 12 criaturas

**Adjunta como referencia de estilo:** `mago_hoja1.png`, `torre_hoja1.png` y `enemigos1.png`
**Formato:** 3:2 horizontal, cuadrícula de 6 columnas × 4 filas (24 celdas). Cada criatura ocupa 2 celdas seguidas (fotograma A y B).

```
Same art style as the attached reference sheets (original cartoon fantasy game art, bold
thick dark-purple outline, vivid saturated colors, glossy painted shading, light from
top-left). Plain solid magenta #FF00FF background.

UNIT SPRITE SHEET: 6 columns x 4 rows of equal cells separated by thin dark-purple grid
lines. Each UNIT uses TWO ADJACENT CELLS (left cell = frame A, right cell = frame B), so each
row holds 3 units. Every unit is an ALLIED, friendly-looking summoned creature, chibi-
proportioned (big head, small body), facing RIGHT, centered and filling about 80% of its
cell, nothing touching the cell edges. Frame A and B are the same creature in two animation
poses (other leg forward, wings up vs down, squashed vs stretched, idle vs active). NO
letters, NO numbers, NO labels, NO text, NO ground shadows.

Row 1: [1] STONE GOLEM: squat mossy stone golem with a small head and big fists, slow and sturdy |
       [2] MUMMY: friendly bandaged mummy with a ragged cloth tail and glowing teal eyes |
       [3] VAMPIRE BAT: a small flying vampire bat-man with big wings, fangs and a tiny cape (A = wings up, B = wings down)
Row 2: [4] GUARDIAN ANGEL: tiny white-and-gold angel with a halo and feathered wings, holding a small glowing spear (A = wings up, B = wings down) |
       [5] SPECTRAL WOLF: translucent blue-white ghost wolf running, glowing eyes, wispy tail |
       [6] TAMED RAPTOR: small green-and-orange raptor dinosaur with a saddle blanket, sharp claws
Row 3: [7] TREANT: small walking tree creature with a bark face, branch arms and leaves on top |
       [8] ABOMINATION: huge stitched-together flesh monster with mismatched arms, bolts and a big jaw |
       [9] PANDA CUB: cute rolling panda cub with a tiny red headband (A = standing, B = curled up rolling)
Row 4: [10] WAR BOAR: tusked brown boar with a spiked armor plate, charging |
       [11] ALPHA WOLF: giant grey-white wolf with scarred muzzle, glowing yellow eyes and a thick mane |
       [12] LARVA: chubby cream-colored grub with a tiny face, crawling (A = stretched, B = squashed)
```
