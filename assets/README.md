## Ilustración anterior del bosque

Archivo: `bosque-verde.png` (1254 × 1254 px). Creado con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`; se conserva como versión anterior del fondo del mapa. Las imágenes del juego se guardan dentro de este repositorio. No requiere servicios externos al jugar.

Prompt final:

> Create a production game background asset, stylized-concept. A lush green woodland clearing viewed from directly overhead with a slight Warcraft-like hand-painted fantasy RTS aesthetic, rich emerald grass, olive moss, little clover patches, scattered small white and blue wildflowers, delicate fern clusters, a few embedded gray stones and worn fragments of ancient stone paving. The middle 65 percent is very open low-contrast grass suitable for displaying many small moving game characters clearly. More detailed vegetation, leafy trees, soft green bushes, mossy rocks and a tiny pool near the outer edges. Subtle diffuse daylight, no heavy cast shadows, no horizon, no sky, no perspective vanishing point. Square terrain texture map filling the entire image, beautiful natural irregular shapes, readable at gameplay scale, refined painted details. No characters, no buildings, no tower, no eggs, no interface, no text, no letters, no logos. The outer edges should be grassy and softly blendable so this can be repeated across a larger field. Deliver one high-resolution square image.
